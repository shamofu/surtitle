# Monitor work-area fitting

The previous fixed 1024 × 700 logical minimum could not fit the measured primary monitor at 200% scaling: its physical work area was 2560 × 1344, with an observed 26 × 71 window frame. The available client area is 2534 × 1273 physical pixels, or 1267 × 636.5 logical pixels.

`src-tauri/src/window_fit.rs` now derives the minimum and normal window bounds from the current monitor work area and measured outer-minus-inner frame. The normal 1024 × 700 logical minimum remains when it fits. Physical integer calculations cap either dimension when necessary and avoid rounding a fractional logical client dimension beyond the work area. Monitor origins stay physical, including negative coordinates.

The configured window starts hidden, is fitted during setup, and is shown after player initialization. Move, resize, scale and focus events are coalesced for 100 ms before fitting on the UI thread, after Windows completes its own DPI transition. Same-monitor dragging does not force position correction. A changed monitor/work area/scale or oversized client does. Maximized, minimized and fullscreen states are left to the operating system; the next normal-state event reapplies the constraints. This never changes system DPI.

Five deterministic Rust tests cover the measured 200% frame, regular 125% sizing, a smaller work area requiring both minima to shrink, negative monitor origins/top taskbar, and an idempotent transition back to a roomier 100% monitor. They passed on Rust 1.99. Geometry unit tests alone do not prove platform event ordering; the subsequent installed-window checks are recorded below.

The workspace checkpoint immediately after this change passed 553 tests with zero failures and 12 harness entries ignored, including subprocess helpers exercised by parent tests. Formatting and native all-targets/all-features clippy with warnings denied passed. Exact counts and the retained log path are in [validation evidence](evidence/window-fit-2026-10-11.json). The later frozen-source results are recorded in [acceptance](acceptance.md).

Actual platform ordering is now proven in the separate normal installed c geometry candidate. The real Windows Settings transition from 100% to 200% retains the unfinished Unicode phrase and selection while fitting the window. A fresh process at 200%, observed before any helper resize, has physical outer bounds `[0,0,2560,1344]` matching the workarea. A requested 1024×700 logical client is capped to outer `[0,0,2074,1344]`. Moving to the 100% secondary monitor and returning yields `[16,0,2096,1344]`, with input, focus and selection preserved. Every step asserts outer containment and primary-control viewport containment; native clipping and immediate Escape also pass. Reports: [live scale transition](evidence/native-c-dpi-live-96-to-192.json) and [fresh startup/mixed monitors](evidence/native-c-dpi-192.json). The c build precedes the independent restore-boundary fix and is not represented as final package acceptance.
